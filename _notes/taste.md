# Taste for post openers

What the opening image of a post should feel like. The register in `paintings.md` is the record of what has been used; this is the brief for choosing the next one.

**Overall:** understated at a distance, rewarding close inspection. Organic linework, quiet stars, ink, atmospheric storms and nocturnes. A strong charcoal, slate and indigo bias, muted colour, restrained contrast. Sage and cream florals also work. Faithful reproductions of real originals, never a pastiche.

**Rejected:** generic galaxy merchandise, ordinary landscapes, loud geometry or novelty, metal, MMA or tattoo menace, cartoonish AI-looking treatments, and yellow or sepia washes. Eccentric animals can work. No slogans.

**Types that work, with examples already liked:**

- Paintings: Turner's Snow Storm and Staffa, Waterhouse's Magic Circle, Balke's Stetind, Sohlberg, Dahl, Munch, Delacroix, De Morgan, Rossetti, Leighton.
- East Asian ink: Musashi's Bird on Reed and Cormorant, Chen Rong's Nine Dragons, Shōhaku's Dragon and Clouds.
- Japanese woodblock prints: Kuniyoshi's giant whale, Yoshitoshi's wolf on Musashi Plain, Hiroshige's Naruto whirlpools and Sudden Shower, Hokusai's waterfalls, Koson's birds.
- Western engravings and lithographs: Piranesi's Carceri, the Flammarion engraving, Dürer's Four Horsemen and Melencolia I, Beardsley's Peacock Skirt, Mucha's celestial series.
- Textiles and pattern: Morris's Larkspur, Acanthus, Wandle and Strawberry Thief, Dearle's Seaweed, Byzantine, Ottoman, Persian, Chinese and Japanese textiles.
- Book illustration: Rackham, Nielsen, Dulac, Bauer, Goodwin's hippo attacking a boat.
- Astronomy: ESO, NASA/ESA Hubble and NOIRLab images (Omega Centauri, Orion, Carina, Tarantula, Milky Way panoramas, NGC 2264, 1232, 3293, 7129). Usually CC BY 4.0, attribution required.
- Microscopy and minerals: polarised-light micrographs of citric acid, paracetamol, basalt; mineral studies. CC BY where found.
- Scientific and natural-history plates: Haeckel's Acanthometra, siphonophores and jellyfish, Audubon, Redouté roses, Merian insects, Cajal's Purkinje cells, Trouvelot's aurora and zodiacal light.
- Early photography: Marey's bird-flight studies, Blossfeldt's plant forms, Anna Atkins's cyanotypes.
- Manuscripts: Book of Kells Chi Rho page, an alchemical ouroboros.
- Maps: considered and mostly rejected.

**Licences:** public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA. Name the author and licence in the caption for CC works.
